FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Moonshine related to alcohol?

Not at all. Moonshine is an AI companionship company. The name comes from moonlight: the quiet late-night hours when a good conversation matters most.

Is my companion a real person?

No. Every Moonshine companion is an AI, and it will always say so if you ask. No humans read your conversations.

What happens to my data?

Your conversations are encrypted, never sold, and never used for ads. You can export or permanently delete your data from settings at any time.

Can I change my companion's personality later?

Yes. You can adjust personality traits, voice, boundaries and check-in frequency whenever you like.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel in two taps and keep access until the end of your billing period.
